1. What NIAT Actually Is (Under 100 Words)

NIAT (NxtWave Institute of Advanced Technologies) is a 4-year on-campus software engineering upskilling model for Class 12 graduates. It is not an independent accredited university. Instead, NIAT embeds inside UGC-recognized partner institutions (e.g., Chaitanya, Malla Reddy) which confer the formal B.Tech degree, while NxtWave delivers intensive, project-heavy industry tech curricula (Full-Stack, AI/ML, 50+ projects) and dedicated placement infrastructure on campus.

2. Six Existing Channels Operated Today
1. YouTube & Vlogs: Top-of-funnel student discovery resolving stigmas.
2. Offline College Seminars: Direct school roadshows in AP/Telangana with NAT tests.
3. Regional Creators: Tech influencers delivering regional affinity (Telugu/Hindi).
4. Free Coding Bootcamps: High-intent lead magnets capturing student contacts.
5. Regional Tele-Counselling: Multilingual inside sales explaining fees & campus visits.
6. Meta Retargeting Ads: Deadline-driven retargeting campaigns for NAT registration.

4. The Hidden Detail (Why Funnels Leak)

The Drop-Off is Parental Consensus, Not Student Aspiration. Students discover NIAT on YouTube with high excitement. However, enrollments freeze at the family consensus stage (10 PM to 1 AM around the kitchen table). Parents in Tier-2/3 towns are terrified of unaccredited private degrees and ambiguous ₹8L–₹18L fee structures. Because admissions counsellors are offline at night, unaddressed skepticism turns into cold rejection before morning.

Layer 2: Track B — Bottom-of-Funnel Conversion Play

The NIAT Parent Decision Confidence System
Candidate: Sumit Bahl
Track: Conversion & Revenue Velocity
1. The New Avenue: Asynchronous AI Family Decision Engine

Instead of relying exclusively on high-pressure 9-to-6 tele-callers, we introduce an always-on, vernacular AI Decision & Confidence Engine deployed directly via WhatsApp. Stalled leads receive an interactive link allowing parents to ask raw, unfiltered financial, accreditation, and hostel safety questions in their native mother tongue (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, English) during late-night kitchen-table deliberations.

2. Why NIAT Specifically? (The Strategic Fit)

NIAT admissions are collective household decisions. In Tier-2/3 cities, parents hold veto power over ₹10L+ educational spend. Parents distrust sales pitches and fear unaccredited certifications. An objective, AI-powered system that explicitly explains UGC partner degree validity, itemizes the ₹8L–₹18L fee tiers, and provides audio synthesis in regional languages transforms hesitation into trust without friction.

Success Threshold: Statistically significant lift of ≥ 3 percentage points in paid admissions within 30 days (e.g. 20% to ≥ 23%), alongside a 40% reduction in sales cycle velocity.

Three Connected Production Components

Component 1: Multilingual AI Family Advisor (Front-End & API)
Deployed on the homepage with 6 Indian languages (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, English). Features Web Speech API voice synthesis, strict XML delimiter injection defense, and resilient dual-fallback model routing (Gemini 2.5 Flash → Pro → Flash-Lite).

Component 2: Real-Time Intent & Objection Classifier
Automatically categorizes parent inquiries into five core buckets (cost, trust, alternatives, timeline, general) and scores purchasing intent from 1 (curious) to 5 (urgent/high-intent). Features Indian 10-digit mobile number validation and atomic rate limiting.

Component 3: Growth & Counsellor Operations Dashboard
Secure HMAC session-authenticated dashboard displaying real-time KPIs, category distribution, and a 🔥 High-Intent Priority Queue (Intent ≥ 4). Enables 1-click regional counsellor assignment, WhatsApp direct-outreach, 30-second auto-masked phone reveal for PII privacy, and safe/unmasked CSV data exports.

Automated Verification Suite

16 Automated Tests Passing (100%): Covers cryptographic HMAC session signing, timing-safe password comparison, brute-force IP rate limiting, phone PII masking, XML prompt injection boundaries, and input schema sanitization. Zero hardcoded secrets in source code or git history.
