1. The New Avenue: Asynchronous AI Family Decision Engine

Instead of relying exclusively on high-pressure 9-to-6 tele-callers, we introduce an always-on, vernacular AI Decision & Confidence Engine deployed directly via WhatsApp. Stalled leads receive an interactive link allowing parents to ask raw, unfiltered financial, accreditation, and hostel safety questions in their native mother tongue (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, English) during their late-night kitchen-table deliberations.

2. Why NIAT Specifically? (The Strategic Fit)

NIAT admissions are collective household decisions, not individual impulse purchases. In Tier-2/3 cities, fathers and elders hold veto power over a ₹10L+ educational spend. Parents distrust aggressive sales pitches and fear unaccredited certifications. An objective, AI-powered system that explicitly explains UGC partner degree validity, itemizes the ₹8L–₹18L fee tiers, and provides audio synthesis in their regional language transforms hesitation into trust without friction.

Pipeline Mechanics: Out of 1,000 top-of-funnel leads, ~800 stall. Historical survey data indicates 60% (~480 families) stall due to unresolved cost or degree validity questions. By capturing and addressing this cohort at the exact moment of decision, converting just 10% of stalled leads adds 48 to 80 high-ticket admissions per cohort cycle.

4. The Live Growth Experiment

Cohort Setup: Identify 1,000 verified leads who took the NAT test or engaged on WhatsApp but went inactive for >72 hours.

Test Group (500 Leads): Receive an automated WhatsApp trigger: "We know choosing a college is a big family decision. Use our official vernacular Family Advisor to verify fees, university degrees, and placements with zero pressure."

Control Group (500 Leads): Receive standard outbound tele-caller reminder phone calls.

Success Threshold: A statistically significant lift of ≥ 3 percentage points in paid admissions within 30 days (e.g., control 20% vs. test ≥ 23%), alongside a 40% reduction in sales cycle velocity.
